Privacy Policy — SessionDock

Last updated: 2026-10-31

What SessionDock does

SessionDock saves the browser tabs you choose to save as named sessions, and restores them when you ask. It also takes automatic backup snapshots of your open tabs so a saved session is never lost.

What data is stored

When you save a session, SessionDock records, for each tab in that session:

  • the page URL and page title;
  • the tab’s position, and whether it was pinned, muted, or unloaded (discarded);
  • the name, colour, and collapsed state of the tab group it belonged to, if any;
  • the tab’s favicon address, if one was available.

It also stores the settings you choose (automatic backup interval, restore target, and whether restored tabs are opened unloaded) and the automatic backup snapshots themselves.

Where the data is stored

On this device only, in the browser’s extension storage (chrome.storage.local for sessions and snapshots; chrome.storage.sync for settings only, so your settings follow your browser profile).

What is never collected or sent

SessionDock makes no network requests of any kind. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising. Your saved tabs never leave your device.

It does not read page content, cookies, passwords, or your browsing history beyond the tabs you explicitly save. It never saves tabs from incognito windows.

How to delete your data

  • Delete a single session from the popup or the dashboard.
  • Delete all sessions from the Settings page (“Danger zone”) or export them first as a JSON file.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all data stored by the extension from the device.
